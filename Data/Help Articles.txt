# How to Track Your Order: Once your order ships, you will receive a confirmation email containing a carrier tracking number. You can also log into your AuraTech account, navigate to "My Orders," and click "Track Package" next to the relevant purchase. Tracking updates may take up to 24 hours to reflect on the carrier's website.
# Initiating a Return: To return an item, visit the "Orders" section in your account, select the order, and click "Request Return." Print the prepaid shipping label provided and attach it to your original packaging. Drop the package off at any authorized shipping location.
# Understanding the Warranty Policy: All AuraTech hardware comes with a 1-year limited warranty covering manufacturing defects. Physical damage, liquid damage, and normal battery depletion are not covered. You can extend this coverage to 3 years by purchasing AuraProtect within 60 days of your original device purchase.
# Bluetooth Pairing Guide for AuraBuds: Open the AuraBuds charging case near your unlocked AuraPhone. A pairing prompt will automatically appear on your screen. Tap "Connect." To pair with a non-Aura device, press and hold the button on the back of the case until the LED flashes white, then select AuraBuds in your device's Bluetooth settings.
# Optimizing Battery Health: To extend the lifespan of your device's lithium-ion battery, avoid exposing it to extreme temperatures (above 95°F / 35°C). Enable "Optimized Battery Charging" in your settings, which learns your daily routine and waits to finish charging past 80% until you need it.
# Cleaning Your Devices: Unplug all cables and turn off your device. Use a soft, slightly damp, lint-free cloth (like a microfiber cloth). Do not use window cleaners, household cleaners, aerosol sprays, solvents, ammonia, or abrasives. For hard-to-remove debris in ports, use a soft-bristled brush.
# Water and Dust Resistance Ratings: The AuraPhone 15 series is rated IP68 under IEC standard 60529 (maximum depth of 6 meters up to 30 minutes). Splash, water, and dust resistance are not permanent conditions and resistance might decrease as a result of normal wear. Liquid damage is not covered under warranty.
# Firmware Update Troubleshooting: If your device fails to update, ensure it is connected to Wi-Fi and has at least 50% battery (or is plugged into power). If the update stalls, force restart your device by quickly pressing Volume Up, Volume Down, and holding the Power button until the logo appears, then try again.
# AuraTech Trade-In Program: You can trade in your old smartphone, tablet, or smartwatch for credit toward your next purchase. Get an estimate online, ship your device for free using our trade-in kit, and receive credit on your original payment method or an AuraTech Gift Card once the device is inspected.
# International Shipping and Customs: We ship to over 50 countries. International shipping rates are calculated at checkout. Please note that customs duties, taxes, and import fees are the responsibility of the recipient and are not included in the checkout total unless explicitly stated at checkout under "Prepaid Duties."
