# AuraTech Returns & Shipping Policy


# Shipping Information


## Processing Times
All standard orders are processed and shipped within 1–2 business days. Orders placed before 2:00 PM (EST) on business days are dispatched the same day. Orders are not shipped or delivered on weekends or public holidays.


## Shipping Rates & Delivery Estimates
- **Standard Shipping:** $5.99 (Free on orders over $50). Delivery in 3–5 business days.
- **Express Shipping:** $14.99. Delivery in 1–2 business days.
- **International Shipping:** $25.00 flat rate. Delivery in 7–14 business days. 


*Note: Delivery delays can occasionally occur due to carrier capacity or extreme weather conditions.*


---


# Returns & Refunds Policy


## 30-Day Return Window
We offer a straightforward, 30-day return policy. If you are not completely satisfied with your purchase, you have 30 days from the date of delivery to request a return. The items must be shipped back to our facility within 14 days of the return request being approved.


## Conditions for Return
To be eligible for a return, your item must be in the same condition that you received it. It must also be in the original packaging, including all accessories, manuals, and protective films.


## Exceptions & Non-Returnable Items
The following items cannot be returned under our standard 30-day policy:
- **In-ear headphones (AuraBuds Series):** Due to strict hygiene standards, in-ear headphones cannot be returned once the protective factory seal has been broken, unless they are defective out-of-the-box.
- **Gift Cards:** All digital and physical gift cards are final sale and non-refundable.
- **Customized/Engraved Items:** Products with personalized engravings cannot be returned or exchanged.
- **Opened Software:** Any physical software media that has been opened or downloaded digital software keys are strictly non-refundable.


# Refunds
Once your return is received and inspected (typically within 3 business days of arrival), we will send you an email to notify you of the approval or rejection of your refund. If approved, the credit will automatically be applied to your original method of payment within 5–7 business days. 


## Late or Missing Refunds
If you haven’t received a refund after 7 business days, first check your bank account again, then contact your credit card company or bank, as processing times vary. If you’ve done all of this and still have not received your refund, please contact us at support@auratech.example.com.
