# About Us – AuraTech Electronics

Founded in 1980, AuraTech Electronics began as a small electronics retailer in Jordan with a clear vision: to make innovative technology accessible to everyone. Over the past four decades, we have grown from a local electronics shop into a trusted regional brand, recognized for our commitment to quality, customer satisfaction, and technological advancement. Our journey has been driven by a passion for electronics and a dedication to helping individuals, families, and businesses find the right technology to meet their needs.

Today, AuraTech operates 23 branches across Jordan, Syria, Saudi Arabia, and France, serving a diverse customer base through our physical stores and digital shopping platforms. Our extensive network allows us to bring the latest electronic products closer to our customers while maintaining consistent service standards across all our locations. From our headquarters in Amman, Jordan, we coordinate our regional operations, product distribution, customer support, and business development initiatives to ensure a seamless shopping experience.

At AuraTech, we offer a comprehensive selection of consumer electronics, including smartphones, laptops, desktop computers, televisions, gaming consoles, smart home devices, audio equipment, accessories, and home appliances. We collaborate with leading international manufacturers and technology brands to provide authentic products, competitive prices, and reliable warranties. Our knowledgeable sales teams help customers compare products, understand technical specifications, and make informed purchasing decisions based on their preferences and budgets.

Throughout our history, AuraTech has continuously invested in improving its operations and customer experience. Our achievements include recognition through regional retail excellence awards, customer service distinctions, and industry acknowledgments for our contribution to the electronics sector. These honors reflect our ongoing commitment to professional service, product quality, and responsible business practices. We also prioritize employee development, ensuring that our teams remain informed about emerging technologies, new product releases, and evolving customer expectations.

Innovation remains central to our future. AuraTech continues to modernize its retail operations through digital transformation, intelligent product recommendations, improved inventory management, and convenient online shopping services. By combining traditional customer care with modern technology, we aim to make every interaction efficient, transparent, and personalized.

Our mission is to empower customers through reliable technology, exceptional service, and accessible innovation. Our vision is to become one of the most trusted electronics retailers across the markets we serve, building lasting relationships with customers, employees, suppliers, and business partners. At AuraTech Electronics, we believe technology should make life simpler, more connected, and more productive. After more than 45 years in business, we remain committed to our founding principles while continually exploring new opportunities to shape the future of electronics retail.
